Aditya - Frames of Innocence

No plan. No setup. Just a kid, his world, and pure chaos in between. It wasn’t easy - running around, distractions, and a lot of treats to win his attention. But somewhere between the noise and the patience, the real moments showed up.

Curiosity. Joy. Innocence. Unscripted. Uncontrolled. Real. And that’s what made every frame worth it.

Shot on Canon R6 Mark II • Lens: 50mm f/1.8 & 85mm f/2 • Edited in Lightroom
