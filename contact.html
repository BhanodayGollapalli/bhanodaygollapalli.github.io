About Me

Hi, I’m Bhanoday Gollapalli, a photographer based in Andhra Pradesh.

I didn’t get into photography to stage things or make everything look perfect. I’m more interested in what’s real — the small moments, natural expressions, and things that happen without planning.

I’m a bit of a quiet person, and I think that reflects in how I work. I don’t like making people feel uncomfortable in front of the camera. I prefer keeping things simple so you can just be yourself.

Most of what I shoot is about emotion, light, and everyday life. If that’s what you’re looking for, feel free to reach out.
