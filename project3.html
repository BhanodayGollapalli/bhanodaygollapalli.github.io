RUSHIKONDA BEACH

This series was shot during golden hour at Rushikonda Beach, Vizag, using natural sunset light to create warm tones and soft highlights.

The focus was on relaxed portraits and candid moments, capturing real expressions without heavy posing.

Shoot with friends, the session had a natural and easy vibe, which reflects in every frame. The changing light added depth, from warm golden tones to subtle silhouettes, bringing out the mood of the beach and the moment.
