ODALAREVU BEACH

Shot during sunrise at Odalarevu Beach, this series captures soft golden light, relaxed portraits, and a few candid moments with a pet dog. The mix of calm atmosphere and playful energy adds a natural balance to the story.

Shot on Canon R6 Mark II • Lens: 50mm f/1.8 & 85mm f/2 & 55-210mm f/5-7.1 • Edited in Lightroom
