Anklet Ceremony

The First Anklet Ceremony is a beautiful tradition celebrated for young girls as they step into a new phase of life. During this ritual, anklets are worn with blessings, symbolizing grace, protection, and prosperity. It is a meaningful occasion where family, culture, and love come together to mark a special beginning.
