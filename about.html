THE PERSON BEHIND THE FRAMES

HELLO, I'M BHANODAY

I see the world
one frame at a time.

I’m a photographer who loves capturing people as they are — the quiet confidence of a portrait, the energy of a place, and the moments in between that often go unnoticed.

My approach is simple: keep things thoughtful, make people feel comfortable, and let the light and the moment do their work. Whether it’s a portrait session or a creative personal project, I want the photographs to feel like you, not just look good.

Bhanu Lens Craft is my space to share that point of view.
