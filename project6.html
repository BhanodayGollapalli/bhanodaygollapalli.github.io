DHEEKSHITHA

Not every shoot goes as planned… and that’s the beauty of working with kids 💛 This little princess wasn’t in the mood for photos today - lots of tears, tiny tantrums, and a whole lot of emotions. But with a little patience, some chocolates, and gentle moments in between, we managed to capture some truly beautiful frames.

Sometimes, the best photos aren’t about perfect poses - they’re about real expressions, real feelings, and real memories. And honestly, those are the moments that matter the most ✨

Shot on Canon R6 Mark II • Lens: 85mm f/2 • Edited in Lightroom
